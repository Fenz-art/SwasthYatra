# SWASTHYATRA

## Founder Blueprint (Pre-Loss Architecture)

### The Complete System We Agreed To Build

---

# Executive Summary

SwasthYatra is a Healthcare Navigation Operating System.

The mission is not:

* Finding doctors
* Booking appointments
* Generic AI healthcare chat

The mission is:

Help travelers navigate healthcare anywhere in the world.

The user should be able to:

* Understand what to do
* Find healthcare
* Communicate across languages
* Share health history
* Get medication equivalents
* Access providers
* Continue care
* Improve outcomes

through a single operating system.

Most healthcare products are:

1. Company Mission

SwasthYatra is building the world's first:

Global Healthcare Navigation Layer

We are not building:

❌ AI Doctor

❌ Doctor Marketplace

❌ Appointment Booking App

❌ Telemedicine Platform

❌ Medical Tourism Portal

We are building:

✅ Healthcare Navigation Infrastructure

for anyone who is outside their normal healthcare network.

2. Origin Story

The company began from the Razorpay Rize problem statement:

Travelers who fall ill in unfamiliar cities don't know which providers to visit, whether doctors speak their language, or what treatment costs to expect.

Initially this appeared to be:

Doctor Discovery Problem

However extensive Reddit research across:

Backpackers
Digital Nomads
Long-Term Travelers
Solo Travelers
Medical Tourists

revealed something surprising.

Travelers rarely begin with doctors.

Their actual workflow is:

Symptoms
↓
Self Assessment
(ChatGPT / Google / Prior Experience)
↓
Pharmacy
↓
Translation Tool
↓
Medication
↓
Clinic / Hospital if worse

This insight changed the company.

3. The Fundamental Discovery

Travelers do not have a diagnosis problem.

They have a navigation problem.

They are not asking:

"What disease do I have?"

They are asking:

"What should I do next?"

This became the foundation of SwasthYatra.

4. Real User Pain Points
Pain 1 — Medication Navigation

Most common issue.

Travelers know:

Cold
Fever
Diarrhea
Food Poisoning

But they do not know:

Local brands
Active ingredients
Prescription rules
OTC availability

Example:

Crocin
Tylenol
Panadol
Calpol
Bufferin

All map to similar active ingredients.

Travelers struggle to navigate this.

Pain 2 — Language Barrier

Current solution:

Google Translate

Problems:

Medical terminology
Allergies
Medication names
Prescription instructions

are often lost.

Pain 3 — Escalation Uncertainty

Users don't need diagnosis.

They need:

Rest?
Pharmacy?
Clinic?
Hospital?
Emergency?

The uncertainty is the pain.

Pain 4 — Trust

Travelers don't know:

Which pharmacy
Which doctor
Which hospital
Which telemedicine service

to trust.

Trust matters more than discovery.

Pain 5 — Medication Continuity

Repeatedly observed.

Examples:

Lost medication
Ran out of medication
Need refill
Country restrictions
Equivalent medicine abroad
